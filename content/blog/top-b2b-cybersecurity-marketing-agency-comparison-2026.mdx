## How We Compare Agencies

Every agency receives a score out of 5.0 under our published weighted rubric:

- **Verified client feedback (30%):** independent reviews on platforms like Clutch, G2, and DesignRush
- **Documented results (25%):** named clients and concrete metrics, not vague "we drove growth" claims
- **Cybersecurity focus (20%):** how much of the agency's work is security, and how deep the technical understanding runs
- **Service breadth (15%):** which channels the agency delivers in-house, and how well they join up
- **Market presence (10%):** startup-to-enterprise range, team scale, and geographic coverage across the US, UK, and Europe

The comparison reads differently depending on which channel drives your pipeline. Each channel pick goes to the highest-scoring agency with that channel as a core service:

- **SEO, content, and AI visibility:** Content Visit (4.9/5) holds all three picks. Hop AI and NOLA Marketing also work here, with different emphases (paid integration and long-standing tech focus respectively).
- **PPC and paid performance:** Hop AI (3.8/5) is the pick, running paid, performance-led media programmes for cybersecurity brands.
- **Enterprise PR and analyst relations:** Highwire (3.5/5) is the pick. Eskenzi PR and specialist firms such as Touchdown PR also cover analyst relations and security-focused media work across the UK, US, and Europe.
- **Positioning and messaging:** Everclear (3.3/5) and Ronin (2.2/5) focus on differentiation and brand for security companies.
- **Demand generation and lead generation:** Envy (3.0/5) is the lead generation pick; Envy and Ronin work on pipeline and go-to-market, with Envy weighted toward early-stage startups.
- **Thought leadership and content at scale:** Highwire produces enterprise long-form content and thought leadership programmes.

## How to Choose From the Comparison

Start with your biggest gap:

1. **Identify your primary channel.** Organic search, paid media, PR, positioning, or demand generation. Start with the channel pick, then shortlist the other agencies active in that channel from the table above.
2. **Weigh stage and budget.** Seed and Series A companies usually need compounding organic value or fast paid pipeline on a smaller retainer, plus positioning help if the product story is not yet clear. Enterprises prioritise analyst relations, global PR, and multi-region reach.
3. **Verify documented results.** Ask each shortlisted agency for cybersecurity-specific case studies with named clients and concrete metrics. Cross-check third-party platforms like Clutch, G2, and LinkedIn.
4. **Check service fit, not service lists.** A long service list with shallow delivery is worth less than a focused specialist who does your priority channel well in-house.
