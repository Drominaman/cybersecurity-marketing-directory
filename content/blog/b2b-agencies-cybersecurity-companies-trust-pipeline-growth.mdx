"Sustained" is the operative word. Plenty of agencies can produce a lead spike: a webinar push, a paid sprint, a content burst that gets a client through one quarter. Fewer can produce pipeline that keeps compounding after the campaign ends, month over month, without a bigger and bigger media budget behind it. For a cybersecurity company, that distinction matters more than most B2B categories, because the sales cycle is long enough (commonly 3 to 9 months for mid-market security tools, 9 to 18 months for enterprise platforms) that a short-term spike is often gone before it ever converts to revenue.

This shortlist covers the agencies in our directory with genuine, documented pipeline work: named clients, specific metrics, and a track record that extends beyond a single case study. **We do not crown a single leader.** The right fit depends on your stage, your primary channel, and your budget. Compare the agencies below on their documented work, not on their position in this list.

## What "Trust" Actually Means Here

Trust in this context is not a feeling. It is a specific, checkable set of things:

- **Named clients, not "a leading security vendor."** If an agency will not name who they worked with, the work either did not happen or did not go well enough to reference.
- **Metrics with a denominator.** "Significant growth" tells you nothing. "MQLs at under £50" or "3x ROI versus paid ad spend" tells you something, because you can compare it to your own numbers.
- **Results that hold up over more than one engagement.** A single great case study can be a fluke. A pattern across multiple named clients, ideally at different stages, is closer to a repeatable method.
- **A channel mix that compounds.** Pipeline that depends entirely on ongoing ad spend stops the moment the budget does. Pipeline built on organic search, content, and structured demand generation keeps producing after the initial investment.

## The Shortlist

### Content Visit

[Content Visit](/agency/content-visit) works exclusively with cybersecurity companies and documents pipeline outcomes across multiple named clients, not just one flagship account. For SenseOn, they produced MQLs at under £50 each with multiple SQLs converted from a single four-week campaign sprint. For Morphisec, the documented output is 180+ marketing qualified leads per quarter through targeted content campaigns, a genuinely repeatable, ongoing number rather than a one-time result. For IBM Security, the work delivered 340% organic search traffic growth alongside 2.5x qualified lead growth in 12 months, and for IronVest, 3x ROI versus equivalent paid ad spend within 6 months.

What makes this relevant to "sustained" specifically is the channel mix: SEO and content marketing that compound over time, paired with lead generation and AI visibility (GEO) so the pipeline is not solely dependent on a paid budget staying switched on. Engagements start at $3,000 per month.

**Well suited to:** companies that want organic pipeline that keeps producing after the initial content investment, rather than a channel that goes quiet the moment spend stops.

Named clients include Check Point, BrandShield, Nuvei, Wing Security, and CyberInt.

Envy's positioning is rooted in the Israeli cybersecurity ecosystem, which shortens the ramp time for security vendors with founder or investor ties there.

**Well suited to:** Series A and B security startups that need pipeline and the operational plumbing (CRM, lead scoring, sales handoff) behind it built at the same time, particularly those with ties to the Israeli ecosystem.

### Ronin

[Ronin](/agency/ronin) positions itself explicitly as an integrated GTM agency "delivering qualified leads for cybersecurity vendors," with Demand Generation and Marketing Strategy listed alongside Brand Strategy and Visual Identity. Their team has in-house experience at major cybersecurity vendors, which shows up in campaigns that read as built by people who have sat inside a security company's own funnel, not a generalist agency guessing at it.

Ronin has not published named-client case studies with specific metrics in the way Content Visit and Envy have, so verify their documented results directly before committing budget.

**Well suited to:** companies whose biggest pipeline blocker is a brand and positioning problem as much as a channel problem, where demand generation needs to be paired with a sharper story to convert.

## What to Ask Before You Sign

1. **Ask for a metric with a time window, not just a number.** "180 MQLs per quarter" is verifiable and repeatable. "We generated hundreds of leads" is not.
2. **Ask what happens to a lead after it is generated.** An agency that stops at form-fills has not solved sustained pipeline; one with a RevOps or lifecycle view has.
3. **Ask for a client at your stage, not just a client the agency is proud of.** A case study from a Series C enterprise platform tells you little about what will happen for a seed-stage startup, and vice versa.
4. **Ask how the channel mix changes if spend or headcount drops.** If the honest answer is "pipeline stops," you are buying a campaign, not sustained growth.
5. **Verify independently.** Check named clients on LinkedIn, and cross-reference any published reviews on Clutch or G2 before you rely on an agency's own account of its results.
